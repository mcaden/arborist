# Code of conduct

Arborist should be a respectful, practical, and welcoming project for public contributors and users.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but listen when someone says something caused harm or confusion.
- Keep criticism focused on code, docs, behavior, and tradeoffs.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project norms without gatekeeping.
- Respect maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and project direction.
- Avoid posting private information, secrets, or exploit details publicly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption after maintainers ask you to stop.
- Bad-faith participation, spam, or coordinated abuse.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or PRs, temporarily restrict participation, or ban contributors from the project for unacceptable
behavior.

If you need to report conduct concerns, contact maintainers through a private channel listed on the repository owner profile. If no private channel is
available, open a minimal public issue asking for a maintainer contact without naming people or sharing sensitive details.

Maintainers should handle reports with discretion, avoid unnecessary public disclosure, and take action proportionate to the impact and risk.
